# OfferCheck — Career Opportunity Risk Assessment Platform

> Verify career opportunities before you trust them.

OfferCheck is a web-based platform designed to help students evaluate internship, training, placement, and job opportunities for potential risk indicators before sharing personal information or making payments.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students increasingly receive internship, training, placement, and job opportunities through websites, email, WhatsApp, and social media.

Some opportunities may contain warning signs such as:

- Unexpected upfront payment requests
- Guaranteed job or placement claims
- Urgency and limited-seat pressure
- Requests for sensitive information
- Suspicious or unusual website URLs
- Unclear selection processes
- Unrealistic earning claims

Students often do not know how to evaluate these signals before making a decision.

## 💡 Solution

OfferCheck provides an explainable risk assessment workflow.

Users can submit opportunity information such as:

- Opportunity title
- Organization name
- Opportunity type
- Opportunity message/email
- Website URL
- Contact information
- Payment amount

The platform evaluates the submitted information and presents potential risk indicators together with recommended verification actions.

## ✨ Key Features

### Opportunity Analyzer
Analyze internship, training, placement, and job opportunities.

### Explainable Risk Assessment
Instead of displaying only a score, OfferCheck identifies individual risk indicators and explains why they matter.

### Risk Scoring
Opportunities receive a risk score from 0–100 with an associated risk level.

### Risk Indicators
The analyzer can evaluate signals such as:

- Upfront payment requests
- Sensitive information requests
- Urgency or pressure
- Guaranteed placement claims
- Selection-process concerns
- Unrealistic salary claims
- Suspicious URL patterns

### Verification Recommendations
Users receive practical actions such as independently verifying the organization, checking official websites, requesting written terms, and avoiding unnecessary sharing of sensitive information.

### Evidence-Oriented Analysis
Risk indicators are intended to be supported by information submitted by the user rather than making unsupported accusations.
